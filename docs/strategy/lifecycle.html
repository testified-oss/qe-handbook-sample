Quality Lifecycle

The end-to-end lifecycle for quality assurance in this handbook, from requirements through release validation.

Phases​

  1. Requirements & Review — quality expectations are defined alongside requirements.
  2. Planning — test strategy, coverage targets, and tooling are selected.
  3. Design & Implementation — tests and checks are built into the product.
  4. Execution — automated and manual validation runs.
  5. Reporting — results are captured and shared.
  6. Release & Feedback — release validation and feedback loops into the next cycle.

Principle​

Quality is not a phase — it is embedded across the entire lifecycle. This section describes the universal flow. Project-specific tailoring belongs in /docs/projects.

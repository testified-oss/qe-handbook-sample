# Tooling Guide

Recommended tooling and setup notes for QE across projects.

## Categories

- **Test frameworks** — unit, integration, and E2E frameworks.
- **CI/CD integration** — pipeline tooling for quality gates.
- **Reporting & dashboards** — result visualization and traceability.
- **Collaboration** — issue tracking, documentation, and communication.

## Principle

Tooling should be adoptable, not prescriptive. Projects may select alternatives; general recommendations here are starting points, not mandates.
